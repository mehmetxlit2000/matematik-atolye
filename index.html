Üslü Sayılar

$$a^n = \underbrace{a \cdot a \cdots a}_{n \text{ tane}}$$
$$a^1 = a, \qquad a^0 = 1 \;\; (a \neq 0), \qquad 1^n = 1, \qquad 0^n = 0 \;\; (n > 0)$$
$$a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}, \qquad \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^{-n} = \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^n, \qquad a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}$$
$$a^n \cdot a^m = a^{n+m}, \qquad \frac{a^n}{a^m} = a^{n-m}$$
$$a^n \cdot b^n = (a \cdot b)^n, \qquad \frac{a^n}{b^n} = \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^n$$
$$(a^n)^m = a^{n \cdot m} = (a^m)^n$$
$$a^x = a^y \implies x = y \quad (a \notin \{-1, 0, 1\})$$
$$x^n = y^n \implies x = y \;\; (n = 2k+1), \qquad x^n = y^n \implies x = \pm y \;\; (n = 2k)$$

Köklü Sayılar

$$a\sqrt[n]{x} \pm b\sqrt[n]{x} = (a \pm b)\sqrt[n]{x}$$
$$\sqrt[n]{a} \cdot \sqrt[n]{b} = \sqrt[n]{a \cdot b}, \qquad \frac{\sqrt[n]{a}}{\sqrt[n]{b}} = \sqrt[n]{\frac{a}{b}}$$
$$\sqrt[n]{a^m} = \sqrt[n \cdot k]{a^{m \cdot k}}, \qquad \sqrt[n]{\sqrt[m]{a}} = \sqrt[n \cdot m]{a}$$
$$\sqrt{a \pm 2\sqrt{b}} = \sqrt{x} \pm \sqrt{y} \quad (x + y = a, \; x \cdot y = b, \; x > y)$$
$$(\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b})(\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}) = a - b$$
$$\sqrt{a + \sqrt{a + \sqrt{a + \dots}}} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{1 + 4a}}{2}$$
$$\sqrt{a - \sqrt{a - \sqrt{a - \dots}}} = \frac{-1 + \sqrt{1 + 4a}}{2}$$
$$\sqrt[n]{x \cdot \sqrt[n]{x \cdot \sqrt[n]{x \cdot \dots}}} = \sqrt[n-1]{x}$$
$$\sqrt{x \div \sqrt{x \div \sqrt{x \div \dots}}} = \sqrt[3]{x}$$

$$|x + y| \le |x| + |y|, \qquad \big||x| - |y|\big| \le |x - y|$$
